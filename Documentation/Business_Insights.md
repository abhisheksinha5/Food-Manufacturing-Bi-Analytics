# Business Insights & Decision Support

## Executive Overview

The executive dashboard provides a high-level view of:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit margin
- Orders
- Target achievement
- Production efficiency

The purpose is to allow management to identify areas requiring further investigation.

## Sales & Growth

The sales module helps identify:

- Revenue contribution by product category
- Regional sales performance
- Customer concentration
- Channel performance
- Salesperson target achievement
- Product growth trends
- Export market contribution

## Operations

The operations module focuses on:

- Production trends
- Production efficiency
- Rejection and wastage
- Inventory position
- Near-expiry stock
- Supplier performance
- Customer complaints

These indicators can be used to prioritize operational investigation.

## Workforce

The workforce module evaluates:

- Productivity per labour hour
- Workforce efficiency
- Present versus required workers
- Production-line performance
- Shift-level performance
- Potential manpower shortages or surpluses

## Decision Framework

The dashboard follows a simple analytical framework:

**What happened?**

→ Identify the KPI or trend.

**Where did it happen?**

→ Drill into product, region, line, shift, customer, or channel.

**Why did it happen?**

→ Investigate supporting operational data.

**What action should be considered?**

→ Use the insight to support business decisions.

The dashboard is intended as a decision-support tool rather than an automated decision-maker.
